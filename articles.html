Questions about sermons & Bible study

How our articles connect to Sunday messages, cell fellowship, and growing in Scripture.

Building community begins with showing up consistently, getting to know people, and taking part in church life beyond Sunday services.

As you become more familiar with the church, consider joining a Cell Fellowship, serving in a ministry, or participating in church activities. These are often the best places to build meaningful relationships and grow in your faith.

At ROLCC, we believe church is more than attending a service—it's about growing together as a community.

ROLCC is a Christ-centred, Bible-believing church committed to "Back to the Bible." We faithfully preach and teach God's Word, proclaim the Gospel of Jesus Christ, and help people grow as His disciples. As a Pentecostal church, we value the work of the Holy Spirit, passionate worship, prayer, and authentic Christian community.

Absolutely. Christian growth is strengthened through encouragement and accountability. At ROLCC, Cell Fellowships, mentoring, and genuine relationships provide opportunities to pray together, encourage one another, and grow faithfully in your walk with Christ.
