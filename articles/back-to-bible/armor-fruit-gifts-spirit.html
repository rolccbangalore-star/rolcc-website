Armor of God, Fruit of the Spirit, Gifts of the Spirit

5 July 2026 · 3 min read

Passage: Ephesians 6:10-18; Galatians 5:22-23; 1 Corinthians 12:7-11

Observe

?? Armor of God | Fruit of the Spirit | Gifts of the Spirit ?? Key Scriptures  Armor of God: Ephesians 6:10-18  Fruit of the Spirit: Galatians 5:22-23  Gifts of the Spirit: 1 Corinthians 12:7-11 ???THE ARMOR OF GOD Purpose: To Stand Key Verse: Ephesians 6:11 &quot;Put on the whole armor of God, that you may be able to stand against the wiles of the devil.&quot; Armor Reference Belt of Truth Ephesians 6:14 Breastplate of Righteousness Ephesians 6:14 Shoes of the Gospel of Peace Ephesians 6:15 Shield of Faith Ephesians 6:16 Helmet of Salvation Ephesians 6:17 Sword of the Spirit Ephesians 6:17 ?? THE FRUIT OF THE SPIRIT Purpose: To Become Like Christ Key Verse: Galatians 5:22-23 &quot;But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace...&quot; Fruit Reference Love Galatians 5:22 Joy Galatians 5:22 Peace Galatians 5:22 Patience Galatians 5:22 Kindness Galatians 5:22 Goodness Galatians 5:22 Faithfulness Galatians 5:22 Gentleness Galatians 5:23 Self-Control Galatians 5:23 ?? THE GIFTS OF THE SPIRIT Purpose: To Serve Others Key Verse: 1 Corinthians 12:7 &quot;The manifestation of the Spirit is given to each one for the profit of all.&quot; Gift Reference Word of Wisdom 1 Corinthians 12:8 Word of Knowledge 1 Corinthians 12:8 Faith 1 Corinthians 12:9 Gifts of Healing 1 Corinthians 12:9 Working of Miracles 1 Corinthians 12:10 Prophecy 1 Corinthians 12:10 Discerning of Spirits 1 Corinthians 12:10 Different Kinds of Tongues 1 Corinthians 12:10 Interpretation of Tongues 1 Corinthians 12:10 ??FUNCTIONAL ASPECTS Aspect Armor of God Fruit of the Spirit Gifts of the Spirit Function Protects Produces Empowers Purpose Stand against the enemy Become like Christ Serve others effectively Focus Spiritual Warfare Spiritual Character Outward Ministry Works On Defense Internal Transformation External Impact Reference Ephesians 6:10-18 Galatians 5:22-23 1 Corinthians 12:7-11 Benefits The Believer The Believer and Those Around Them The Body of Christ Key Question How do I stand? Who am I becoming? How can I serve? Result Victory Maturity Edification ?? A PRACTICAL COMPARISON Imagine a Soldier ???Armor of God - The soldier's equipment. Without armor, he becomes vulnerable. ?? Fruit of the Spirit - The soldier's character. Without character, he becomes unstable. ?? Gifts of the Spirit - The soldier's abilities and assignments. Without gifts, he becomes ineffective. Armor protects him. Fruit shapes him. Gifts use him. ? CONCLUSION Many Christians focus on one area while neglecting the others. God desires believers to be:  Protected through the Armor of God  Transformed through the Fruit of the Spirit  Empowered through the Gifts of the Spirit A mature believer should possess all three. ?? REFLECTION QUESTIONS 1. Which of the three areas do you focus on most? 2. Which area do you tend to neglect? 3. Can someone have spiritual gifts without spiritual maturity? 4. How do the Armor, Fruit, and Gifts work together in the life of a believer? ?? Closing Thought The Armor of God helps us stand. The Fruit of the Spirit helps us grow. The Gifts of the Spirit help us serve.
