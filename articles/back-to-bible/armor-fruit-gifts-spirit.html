Armor of God, Fruit of the Spirit, Gifts of the Spirit

5 July 2026 · 4 min read

Passage: Ephesians 6:10-18; Galatians 5:22-23; 1 Corinthians 12:7-11

Scripture Reading Ephesians 6:10-17 NKJV · Tap to read

New King James Version

10 Finally, my brethren, be strong in the Lord and in the power of His might.

11 Put on the whole armor of God, that you may be able to stand against the wiles of the devil.

12 For we do not wrestle against flesh and blood, but against principalities, against powers, against the rulers of the darkness of this age, against spiritual hosts of wickedness in the heavenly places.

13 Therefore take up the whole armor of God, that you may be able to withstand in the evil day, and having done all, to stand.

14 Stand therefore, having girded your waist with truth, having put on the breastplate of righteousness,

15 and having shod your feet with the preparation of the gospel of peace;

16 above all, taking the shield of faith with which you will be able to quench all the fiery darts of the wicked one.

17 And take the helmet of salvation, and the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God;

Overview

  • Armor of God | Fruit of the Spirit | Gifts of the Spirit
  • Key Scriptures
  • Armor of God: Ephesians 6:10-18
  • Fruit of the Spirit: Galatians 5:22-23
  • Gifts of the Spirit: 1 Corinthians 12:7-11

THE ARMOR OF GOD

  • Purpose: To Stand
  • Key Verse: Ephesians 6:11
"Put on the whole armor of God, that you may be able to stand against the wiles of the devil."

Armor - Reference

  • Belt of Truth, Breastplate of Righteousness - Ephesians 6:14
  • Shoes of the Gospel of Peace - Ephesians 6:15
  • Shield of Faith - Ephesians 6:16
  • Helmet of Salvation, Sword of the Spirit - Ephesians 6:17

THE FRUIT OF THE SPIRIT

  • Purpose: To Become Like Christ
  • Key Verse: Galatians 5:22-23
"But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace..."

Fruit - Reference

  • Love, Joy, Peace, Patience, Kindness, Goodness, Faithfulness - Galatians 5:22
  • Gentleness, Self-Control - Galatians 5:23

THE GIFTS OF THE SPIRIT

  • Purpose: To Serve Others
  • Key Verse: 1 Corinthians 12:7
"The manifestation of the Spirit is given to each one for the profit of all."

Gift - Reference

  • Word of Wisdom, Word of Knowledge - 1 Corinthians 12:8
  • Faith, Gifts of Healing - 1 Corinthians 12:9
  • Working of Miracles, Prophecy, Discerning of Spirits, Different Kinds of Tongues, Interpretation of Tongues - 1 Corinthians 12:10

A PRACTICAL COMPARISON

Imagine a Soldier Armor of God - The soldier's equipment. Without armor, he becomes vulnerable. Fruit of the Spirit - The soldier's character. Without character, he becomes unstable. Gifts of the Spirit - The soldier's abilities and assignments. Without gifts, he becomes ineffective. Armor protects him. Fruit shapes him. Gifts use him.

CONCLUSION

Many Christians focus on one area while neglecting the others. God desires believers to be:  Protected through the Armor of God  Transformed through the Fruit of the Spirit  Empowered through the Gifts of the Spirit A mature believer should possess all three.

REFLECTION QUESTIONS

4How do the Armor, Fruit, and Gifts work together in the life of a believer?

Closing Thought The Armor of God helps us stand. The Fruit of the Spirit helps us grow. The Gifts of the Spirit help us serve.

Functional Aspects of the Fruit and Gifts of the Holy Spirit

The Holy Spirit works in two complementary ways. The Fruit of the Spirit produces spiritual character through internal transformation, helping believers become like Christ (Galatians 5:22–23). The Gifts of the Spirit empower believers for outward ministry, enabling them to serve others effectively and bring edification to the Body of Christ (1 Corinthians 12:7–11).

  • Fruit: "Who am I becoming?" → Become like Christ; Maturity (Galatians 5:22–23).
  • Gifts: "How can I serve?" → Serve others effectively; Edification of the Body of Christ (1 Corinthians 12:7–11).
  • Fruit benefits the believer; gifts benefit the Body of Christ and those around the believer (Galatians 5:22–23; 1 Corinthians 12:7–11).

Discussion questions

  • Which of the three areas — armor, fruit, or gifts — do you focus on most?
  • Which area do you tend to neglect?
  • Can someone have spiritual gifts without spiritual maturity?
  • How do the armor, fruit, and gifts work together in the life of a believer?
