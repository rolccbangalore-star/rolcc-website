Romans 8:1-11 — Life in the Spirit

20 June 2026 · 1 min read

Passage: Romans 8:1-11

Observe

Read Romans 8:1-11 slowly. Notice the contrasts Paul draws: condemnation vs no condemnation, flesh vs Spirit, death vs life and peace.

Interpret

Paul teaches that those in Christ are set free from condemnation. The mind set on the Spirit brings life and peace.

Apply

Where do you feel condemnation or pressure this week? How might setting your mind on the Spirit change how you respond?

Discussion questions

  • What does 'no condemnation' mean for believers in Christ?
  • How does Paul describe the difference between the flesh and the Spirit?
  • What is one practical way to 'set the mind on the Spirit' this week?

Scripture meditation
