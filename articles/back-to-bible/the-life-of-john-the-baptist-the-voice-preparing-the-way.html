The Life of John the Baptist: "The Voice Preparing the Way"

20 July 2026 · 5 min read

Passage: Matthew 3:1-17; John 1:29, 3:30; Luke 1:5-80; Matthew 14:1-12

Scripture Reading Matthew 3:1-6, John 1:29 NKJV · Tap to read

New King James Version

1 In those days John the Baptist came preaching in the wilderness of Judea,

2 and saying, "Repent, for the kingdom of heaven is at hand!"

3 For this is he who was spoken of by the prophet Isaiah, saying: "The voice of one crying in the wilderness: 'Prepare the way of the Lord; Make His paths straight.'"

4 Now John himself was clothed in camel's hair, with a leather belt around his waist; and his food was locusts and wild honey.

5 Then Jerusalem, all Judea, and all the region around the Jordan went out to him

6 and were baptized by him in the Jordan, confessing their sins.

29 The next day John saw Jesus coming toward him, and said, "Behold! The Lamb of God who takes away the sin of the world!"

INTRODUCTION

John the Baptist was the last Old Testament-style prophet and the forerunner of Jesus Christ. His mission was not to build his own ministry, but to prepare people's hearts for the coming Messiah. Jesus said of him in Matthew 11:11 (NKJV): "Assuredly, I say to you, among those born of women there has not risen one greater than John the Baptist..."

FACTS TO KNOW

  • Father: Zechariah (Priest)
  • Mother: Elizabeth
  • Relative of Mary: Elizabeth was Mary's relative (Luke 1:36)
  • King during his birth: Herod the Great
  • Where he lived: Wilderness of Judea
  • Food: Locusts and wild honey
  • Clothing: Camel's hair with a leather belt
  • Mission: Prepare the way for Jesus

1. THE ANGEL APPEARS TO ZECHARIAH

Luke 1:13–17 (NKJV): "But the angel said to him, 'Do not be afraid, Zechariah, for your prayer is heard; and your wife Elizabeth will bear you a son, and you shall call his name John... He will also go before Him in the spirit and power of Elijah...'" While Zechariah was serving in the Temple, the angel Gabriel announced John's upcoming birth. John was chosen before he was even born, specifically tasked with the mission to prepare people for the Messiah. Lesson: God prepares His servants before their ministry begins.

2. ZECHARIAH DOUBTS GOD

Luke 1:18–20: "And behold, you will be mute... because you did not believe my words..." Zechariah questioned God's promise due to their advanced age. Because of his doubt, God made him unable to speak until John was born. Lesson: God's promises do not depend on our faith, but they call us to trust Him.

3. JOHN IS BORN

Luke 1:57–64 When Elizabeth gave birth to a son, everyone assumed he would be named after his father. However, Zechariah in obedience wrote, "His name is John," and immediately his speech was restored. Lesson: Obedience brings blessing.

4. JOHN GROWS IN THE WILDERNESS

Luke 1:80 (NKJV): "So the child grew and became strong in spirit, and was in the deserts till the day of his manifestation to Israel." John spent years in the wilderness where God prepared him in private before revealing him to public ministry. Lesson: God develops character before calling us into service.

5. JOHN PREACHES REPENTANCE

Matthew 3:1–2 (NKJV): "Repent, for the kingdom of heaven is at hand!" John's core message was repentance—he came to prepare hearts rather than physical roads, and many came confessing their sins. Lesson: Revival begins with repentance.

6. JOHN'S SIMPLE LIFESTYLE

Matthew 3:4 (NKJV): "Now John himself was clothed in camel's hair... and his food was locusts and wild honey." John lived simply and remained completely undistracted by worldly comforts or social status. Lesson: God values faithfulness more than luxury.

7. JOHN BAPTIZES JESUS

Matthew 3:13–17 When Jesus came to be baptized, John initially hesitated and refused. Jesus explained that it was necessary to fulfill all righteousness. As Jesus emerged from the water, the Holy Spirit descended like a dove and the Father declared, "This is My beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased." Lesson: The Trinity is clearly revealed at Jesus' baptism.

8. JOHN IDENTIFIES JESUS

John 1:29 (NKJV): "Behold! The Lamb of God who takes away the sin of the world!" John publicly introduced Jesus to his own followers, deliberately pointing people away from himself and toward Christ. Lesson: Every servant of God should point people to Christ.

9. "HE MUST INCREASE"

John 3:30 (NKJV): "He must increase, but I must decrease." When John's disciples grew concerned that crowds were leaving them to follow Jesus, John rejoiced instead of becoming jealous. Lesson: True ministry exalts Christ, not ourselves.

10. JOHN REBUKES HEROD

Matthew 14:3–4 (NKJV): "For John had said to him, 'It is not lawful for you to have her.'" Herod had married Herodias, his brother Philip's wife. John boldly confronted this sin without fear of political status, which ultimately led to his imprisonment. Lesson: God's truth must be preached without compromise.

11. JOHN'S DOUBT

Matthew 11:2–6 While sitting in prison, John sent his disciples to ask if Jesus was truly the Messiah. Jesus answered not with offense, but by pointing directly to fulfilled prophecy and His miraculous works. Lesson: Even faithful believers can struggle, but Jesus strengthens our faith.

12. JOHN'S MARTYRDOM

Matthew 14:6–12 After Herodias' daughter danced before Herod, she requested John's head on a platter. John was beheaded in prison, and his loyal disciples came to bury his body. Lesson: Faithfulness to God is more important than preserving our lives.

ACTIVITIES

Closing Prayer & Reflection: Take a few moments of quiet reflection. Ask the Lord to show you one area of your life where you need Christ to increase and self to decrease. Conclude with a group prayer asking for boldness like John's to point others to the Lamb of God.

Discussion questions

  • Why do you think God chose to prepare John in the solitude of the wilderness before launching his public ministry?
  • How does Zechariah’s initial doubt and subsequent obedience challenge the way we respond to God's promises in our own lives?
  • What does true repentance look like today, and why is it essential for experiencing spiritual revival?
  • John lived a very simple lifestyle focused entirely on his calling. What are some modern distractions that prevent us from keeping Christ at the center?
  • John joyfully declared, "He must increase, but I must decrease." How can we cultivate this same mindset in our jobs, families, and church service?
  • Even after witnessing supernatural events, John wrestled with doubt while in prison. What can we learn from how Jesus responded to John's questions?
