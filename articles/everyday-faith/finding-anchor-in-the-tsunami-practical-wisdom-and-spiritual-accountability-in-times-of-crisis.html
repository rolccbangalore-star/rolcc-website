Finding Anchor in the Tsunami: Practical Wisdom and Spiritual Accountability in Times of Crisis

PrayerFaith

Rev. Benson K Sunny·Psalm 142:4

Summary

When life is shaken by crisis, fear can easily overwhelm us. This message combines biblical wisdom with practical responsibility, showing how faith in God and wise action help us remain steady during difficult seasons.

Look and see, there is no one at my right hand; no one cares for my life.

Psalm 142:4

Major crises remind us how limited human strength can be. God invites us to anchor our hope in Him while acting with wisdom and compassion.

Faith Requires Wise Action

The Passover account teaches that trusting God includes obedient, practical action. Biblical faith is never separated from wisdom.

Psalm 142:4

God remains our refuge even when we feel alone.

Responding with Wisdom

  • Follow sound health guidance.
  • Seek medical help without unnecessary delay.
  • Replace fear with prayer and intercession.
  • Support those who are isolated or suffering.

Is it wrong to feel afraid during a crisis?

No. Like David, we can honestly bring our fears to God in prayer.

What does biblical faith look like?

Faith trusts God while also walking in wisdom and obedience.

How can I stay spiritually strong?

Remain rooted in Scripture, prayer, and Christian community.

We would love to pray with you during this season.

Request Prayer

Key takeaways

  • God is our refuge.
  • Faith and wisdom work together.
  • Prayer redirects fear.
  • Care for others practically.
  • Keep a soft heart before God.
