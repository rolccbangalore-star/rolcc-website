Finding rest when work never stops

Work & Calling ROLCC Pastoral Team 15 June 2026 2 min read Matthew 11:28-30

Summary

Jesus invites the weary to come as they are. Rest is not escape from responsibility — it is a sustainable rhythm of Sabbath, Scripture, and community.

Work in Bangalore can feel unending—deadlines, traffic, notifications, and the pressure to always be available. Jesus' invitation in Matthew 11 is not an escape from responsibility, but a deeper rest for your soul.

Come honestly

You do not need to have everything together before you come to God. Bring your tiredness, your questions, and your unfinished to-do list. He welcomes you as you are.

Learn a sustainable rhythm

Rest is more than a vacation. It includes Sabbath moments, prayer, Scripture, and community. Start with one small step you can repeat this week—a ten-minute pause with the Bible, or an honest conversation with someone you trust.

Share the load

God designed us for community. Cell Fellowship, church family, and serving alongside others help us carry burdens we were never meant to bear alone.

Come to me, all you who are weary and burdened, and I will give you rest.

Matthew 11:28

Matthew 11:28-30

Come to me, all you who are weary and burdened, and I will give you rest. Take my yoke upon you and learn from me, for I am gentle and humble in heart, and you will find rest for your souls.

Rest grows in community. Find a Cell Fellowship group near you.

Join Cell Fellowship

If you are weary today, you are welcome at ROLCC—no performance required. Come as you are, and take your next step at your own pace.

Key takeaways

  • Come to God honestly — you do not need to have everything together first.
  • Build a sustainable rhythm: Sabbath moments, prayer, Scripture, and community.
  • Share the load through Cell Fellowship and church family.
