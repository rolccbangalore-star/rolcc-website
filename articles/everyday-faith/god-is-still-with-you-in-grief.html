God is still with you in grief

Faith & Peace Pastor Samuel 3 July 2026 1 min read Psalm 34:18

Summary

Grief is not a failure of faith. Jesus wept, and He walks with you through sorrow at your pace.

Loss can make church feel loud when your heart feels hollow. You are not alone, and you are not behind spiritually because sorrow takes time.

The Lord is close to the brokenhearted and saves those who are crushed in spirit.

Psalm 34:18
