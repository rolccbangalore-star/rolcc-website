How to Stop Stressing and Trust God with Your Problems

Faith & Peace Pr. Benson K Sunny 6 July 2026 2 min read 2 Chronicles 20:15

Summary

Life's challenges often tempt us to carry every burden on our own. This message reminds us that God calls us to trust Him in every battle, exchanging anxiety for faith as we rely on His strength instead of our own.

Do not be afraid or dismayed because of this great multitude, for the battle is not yours, but God's.

2 Chronicles 20:15

When difficulties arise, we often try to solve everything ourselves. We carry the emotional weight of every outcome, yet once the problem is over, we are tempted to take the credit instead of acknowledging God's faithfulness.

The Battle Belongs to God

Throughout Scripture, God repeatedly demonstrates that victory comes from Him. At Jericho, He brought down the walls without conventional warfare, reminding His people that His power—not human ability—secures the victory.

Gideon's Lesson in Trust

God reduced Gideon's army from thousands to just 300 men so Israel would know that success came from Him alone. By removing every reason for human pride, God taught His people to depend completely on Him.

2 Chronicles 20:15

The battle is not yours, but God's.

Practical Ways to Trust God

  • Write down your fears and prayers before the situation changes to remember God's faithfulness later.
  • Spend time in God's Word and learn to recognize His voice above every other influence.
  • Keep a testimony journal of God's answers to prayer and provision.
  • Do your part faithfully while leaving the outcome in God's hands.

If the battle belongs to God, why do I still face difficulties?

God never promised a life without challenges. He promises to be with us and use every battle to strengthen our faith.

Is it wrong to feel afraid?

Fear is a normal human emotion. The goal is not to let fear control our decisions but to trust God despite it.

What does trusting God look like in daily life?

Trusting God means faithfully doing what you can while surrendering the final outcome to Him.

Grow in faith alongside others as you learn to trust God through every season.

Connect with a Cell Group

Key takeaways

  • The battle ultimately belongs to God.
  • Self-reliance creates unnecessary anxiety and pride.
  • God often removes our confidence in human strength so we learn to trust Him.
  • Remembering God's past faithfulness strengthens future faith.
  • Faith means obeying God while leaving the results in His hands.
