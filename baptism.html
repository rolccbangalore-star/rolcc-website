Why baptism matters

Baptism is not the source of salvation. It is a step of obedience that follows Jesus' example and publicly aligns your life with God's Word. When you have decided to follow Christ, baptism is how you declare that decision before God and His people.

“We were therefore buried with him through baptism into death in order that, just as Christ was raised from the dead through the glory of the Father, we too may live a new life.” Romans 6:4 (NIV)

At River of Life, we would love to walk with you through baptism preparation and the ceremony. Whether you are new to faith or ready to take this public step after years of walking with Jesus, you are welcome.

How baptism works at ROLCC

Simple next steps so you can prepare with clarity and confidence.

01

Believe

Baptism follows faith in Jesus. If you have received Him as Lord and Savior, baptism is the natural next step of obedience.

02

Prepare

Our pastoral team will help you understand what baptism means, answer questions, and walk with you through preparation for the day.

03

Declare

In the ceremony, you publicly declare your faith. Your church family celebrates with you as you begin this new chapter of obedience.

A prayer before baptism

You can pray this as you prepare, or use your own words.

Heavenly Father,

Thank You for the gift of salvation through Jesus. As I prepare for baptism, help me follow You with a sincere heart. Let this step honour You, strengthen my faith, and remind me that I belong to Christ. Give me courage to declare what You have done in my life.

In Jesus' name, Amen.

Baptism at ROLCC

Believer’s baptism by immersion—who it is for, how to prepare, and how it relates to salvation.

No. Salvation is a gift from God through faith in Jesus Christ. Baptism does not save you. It is the public declaration of a decision you have already made to follow Him.

We practise believer's baptism by immersion. Baptism is for those who understand the gospel, have personally placed their faith in Jesus, and want to publicly declare that decision. We do not baptise infants or young children who cannot yet understand and take this step for themselves. Our pastoral team will talk with you during preparation to make sure this step is clear and meaningful.

Contact us and share that you would like to be baptised. We will follow up with preparation details, timing, and pastoral guidance for the ceremony.

Salvation is a gift from God. It cannot be earned. Because of God's love, He sent His Son, Jesus, to die for the sin that separates us from Him, and Jesus rose again in victory over sin and death. We receive this gift by faith in Jesus Christ.

Tell someone you trust at church, keep reading the Bible (start with John), and take your next steps in community—Sunday worship, Cell Fellowship, and baptism when you are ready. Our pastors and Caring Team are glad to walk with you.
