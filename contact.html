Before you visit or reach out

Location, visiting, prayer requests, and how to connect with our pastors.

You can simply walk in. Registration is not required for a regular Sunday service. If you need any assistance, our welcome team will be happy to help you.

Yes. ROLCC is located in HSR Layout, making it easily accessible from Electronic City, Koramangala, Bellandur, Sarjapur Road, BTM Layout, and nearby areas. We'd love to welcome you if you're looking for a Bible-believing church in South-East Bangalore.

Absolutely. We'd be honoured to pray with you.

Whether your request is personal, for your family, work, health, or anything else, our pastors and prayer team are available after every service. You can also share your prayer request with us online or contact the church during the week.

Yes. If you'd like to speak with a pastor, we'd be happy to arrange a private conversation.

Whether you have questions about faith, need spiritual guidance, or simply want someone to listen, we'll do our best to provide a safe, caring, and confidential environment.

Yes. One of the purposes of the church is to support one another through every season of life.

Depending on your situation, this may include prayer, pastoral care, counselling, practical encouragement, or connecting you with a caring church community. We want you to know that you don't have to walk through difficult times alone.

Absolutely. If you're visiting for the first time, there's no expectation for you to know the order of the service, the songs, or when to stand or sit. Simply follow along as you're comfortable, and if you have any questions, our pastors and church family will be happy to help.

Yes. You can attend ROLCC without becoming a member. Many people take time to understand what we believe, experience the community, and ask honest questions before making any commitment. That said, membership can be meaningful—it’s a way of saying “this is my church family,” and it also helps us care well for you. For certain pastoral services like weddings, baby dedication, burial support, and some priority care, members may receive preference (not to exclude others, but because church family carries shared responsibilities and privileges). You’re welcome either way—start by visiting and take your next step when you’re ready.

Yes—please do. Asking someone to pray with you is a simple, biblical way to seek God’s help, and you don’t need to have the “right words.” At ROLCC, you can request prayer through our Prayer Ministry, speak with a pastor after service, or share your need through the Welcome Form so someone can follow up. If you’re comfortable, a Cell Fellowship is also a safe place to be prayed for and supported over time. You’re not meant to carry burdens alone.
