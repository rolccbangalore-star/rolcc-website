Start by serving in one simple place and staying connected to people who can walk with you. Purpose in church isn’t about a “position”—it’s about following Jesus, growing, and using what God has given you to bless others. A practical next step is to join a Cell Fellowship, speak with a pastor or leader, and try one ministry area for a few weeks (welcoming, River Kids, worship, media, outreach, etc.). Over time, your gifts become clearer as you show up, learn, and love people.
