Questions & Answers

Common questions before your first visit

Practical answers if you're planning to visit ROLCC for the first time.

When you visit ROLCC, you can expect a warm welcome, a time of worship, prayer, and a Bible-based message. You're free to participate as much or as little as you're comfortable with.
As a first-time visitor, you'll also receive a thoughtfully prepared welcome kit. It includes information about the church, a handcrafted greeting, and a few small gifts that have been prayerfully prepared as a simple expression of our love and appreciation for your visit.
After the service, you're welcome to meet our pastors or church members, ask questions, or simply head home at your own pace.

You can simply walk in. Registration is not required for a regular Sunday service. If you need any assistance, our welcome team will be happy to help you.

There is no dress code at ROLCC. Wear something modest and comfortable. Our desire is that you feel at ease and can focus on worship and hearing God's Word rather than what you're wearing.

Yes. Everyone is welcome at ROLCC regardless of their faith or background. Whether you're exploring Christianity, visiting with a friend, or simply curious, you're welcome to attend our services without any pressure to become a member or Christian. You're free to observe, ask questions, and take your journey at your own pace.

Romans 15:7

Absolutely. If you're visiting for the first time, there's no expectation for you to know the order of the service, the songs, or when to stand or sit. Simply follow along as you're comfortable, and if you have any questions, our pastors and church family will be happy to help.

Absolutely. You're welcome to attend ROLCC, take your time, and explore the Christian faith without feeling pressured. You're free to listen, ask honest questions, and take your next step as you seek God.

Finding the right church is about more than choosing a place to attend on Sundays—it's about finding a community that helps you grow in your relationship with Jesus.

Look for a church where Christ is at the centre, the Bible is taught faithfully, people genuinely care for one another, and you have opportunities to grow and serve. Visit a few times, ask questions, and prayerfully decide before making it your church home.

At ROLCC, we encourage people to take their time and make an informed decision.

Yes. ROLCC is located in HSR Layout, making it easily accessible from Electronic City, Koramangala, Bellandur, Sarjapur Road, BTM Layout, and nearby areas. We'd love to welcome you if you're looking for a Bible-believing church in South-East Bangalore.
