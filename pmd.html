What We Do

The Pre-Ministry Diploma (PMD) provides comprehensive training across biblical foundations, leadership development, and practical ministry skills. Our goal is to equip believers who sense a call to ministry with the tools and character needed to serve effectively.

  • Biblical training — foundations and hermeneutics
  • Leadership development — character and vision
  • Pastoral training — care and ministry
  • Administrational training — church operations
  • Life Orientation Month (LOM) — February & October

Questions & Answers

Calling, training & everyday ministry

Growing in purpose, mentorship, and serving God while working or studying.

God's calling is often recognised as you grow in your relationship with Him, faithfully serve where you are, and receive confirmation through His Word, prayer, and wise spiritual counsel. At ROLCC, we're committed to helping you discern and develop God's calling through mentoring, ministry training, and practical opportunities to serve.

Absolutely. Many people faithfully serve God while working full-time. At ROLCC, we encourage believers to honour God through their profession, remain active in church life, and use their gifts to serve both in their workplace and through the ministries of the church.

Yes. ROLCC offers ministry training to help believers grow in biblical understanding, leadership, practical ministry, and Christ-like character. Whether you're exploring ministry or preparing to serve, our training is designed to equip you to serve God faithfully and effectively.

Spiritual growth is best nurtured through relationships. At ROLCC, you'll have opportunities to connect with pastors, ministry leaders, and mature believers who can encourage, guide, pray with you, and help you grow in your walk with Christ.

Yes. ROLCC periodically offers internship opportunities for those who want to grow in ministry, leadership, and practical church service. If you're interested, we'd be glad to discuss current opportunities and help you find a pathway that matches your calling, gifts, and stage of growth.

If you're looking for ministry opportunities in Bangalore, we'd love to help you find a place to grow and serve. At ROLCC, there are opportunities across worship, media, River Kids, outreach, hospitality, administration, prayer, discipleship, and many other ministries where you can use your God-given gifts.

Every profession can be an opportunity to honour God. Whether you're a student, business owner, homemaker, artist, engineer, healthcare professional, teacher, or in any other field, you can glorify God by working with integrity, serving others with excellence, and reflecting the character of Christ. At ROLCC, we're passionate about helping people see their workplace as a mission field and their profession as part of God's calling for their lives.

Purpose isn’t usually found in one moment—it’s formed through walking with Jesus and faithfully doing the next right thing. Start with what God clearly calls every believer to: love God, love people, and grow in His Word. Then notice where your gifts and burdens meet real needs. At ROLCC, a practical way to discover this is to join Cell Fellowship, serve in one ministry area for a season (Welcome, Media, Outreach, River Kids, etc.), and ask a pastor/leader for guidance. Clarity often comes while you’re moving, not while you’re stuck waiting.
