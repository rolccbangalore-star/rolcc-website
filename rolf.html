What We Do

River of Life Foundation (ROLF) serves children and families in need through a range of initiatives designed to provide practical help, educational support, and hope in difficult times.

  • Scholarship exams — supporting children's education
  • Academic training — extra help for students who need it
  • Big Box — seasonal outreach such as Christmas gift boxes
  • Package of Help — practical support for families in crisis
  • Year-round initiatives across Bengaluru

Giving, outreach & community impact

Charity, education, CSR partnerships, and serving through ROLF.

If you're looking to make a meaningful difference, ROLF provides opportunities to serve individuals, families, and communities through education, community care, outreach, and other initiatives. Whether you can give your time, skills, or resources, we'd be glad to help you find a meaningful way to serve.

You can make a lasting impact by supporting educational initiatives that help children reach their God-given potential. Through ROLF, you can contribute by sponsoring programmes, volunteering your skills, mentoring students, or partnering with us to provide quality education and opportunities for underprivileged children.

Absolutely. Through ROLF, we welcome partnerships with businesses, organisations, and institutions that share a desire to make a positive impact in the community. We'd be glad to explore CSR initiatives, community projects, educational programmes, and other opportunities to serve together.

Absolutely. Your time, skills, and experience can make a meaningful difference. Whether your expertise is in teaching, media, technology, administration, healthcare, design, finance, mentoring, or another field, we'd be glad to explore how you can serve through ROLCC or ROLF.
