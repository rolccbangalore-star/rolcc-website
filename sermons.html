Questions & Answers

Serving, ministry & pastoral care

Questions about volunteering, joining a ministry team, finding your calling, and connecting with pastoral support or counselling.

The best way to join a ministry is to speak with one of our pastors or ministry leaders after a service or contact the church office.

We'll help you understand the different ministry opportunities, learn about your interests and gifts, and guide you towards the ministry where you can serve effectively.

Yes. We believe serving is both a privilege and a responsibility.

Before serving in many ministries, volunteers receive orientation, guidance, and ongoing training to help them serve with excellence, confidence, and Christ-like character.

Not always.

Some serving opportunities are open to those who are regularly attending and getting to know the church, while other ministries may require additional preparation or church membership. This helps us care well for both our volunteers and those they serve.

Our leaders will guide you through the appropriate next steps based on the ministry you're interested in.

God's calling is often recognised as you grow in your relationship with Him, faithfully serve where you are, and receive confirmation through His Word, prayer, and wise spiritual counsel. At ROLCC, we're committed to helping you discern and develop God's calling through mentoring, ministry training, and practical opportunities to serve.

Yes. One of the purposes of the church is to support one another through every season of life.

Depending on your situation, this may include prayer, pastoral care, counselling, practical encouragement, or connecting you with a caring church community. We want you to know that you don't have to walk through difficult times alone.

Absolutely. We'd be honoured to pray with you.

Whether your request is personal, for your family, work, health, or anything else, our pastors and prayer team are available after every service. You can also share your prayer request with us online or contact the church during the week.

Yes. If you'd like to speak with a pastor, we'd be happy to arrange a private conversation.

Whether you have questions about faith, need spiritual guidance, or simply want someone to listen, we'll do our best to provide a safe, caring, and confidential environment.

Building community begins with showing up consistently, getting to know people, and taking part in church life beyond Sunday services.

As you become more familiar with the church, consider joining a Cell Fellowship, serving in a ministry, or participating in church activities. These are often the best places to build meaningful relationships and grow in your faith.

At ROLCC, we believe church is more than attending a service—it's about growing together as a community.
